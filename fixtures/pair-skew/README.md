# Asymmetric pair qualification article

Agent-authored software routing case, captured from agent-ee on 2026-10-24.
J1 is fixed at (45,50) mm; J2 at (75,70) mm, rotated 90 degrees. Four copper
layers are enabled. The native project declares CAN+ and CAN- with 0.25 mm
width, 0.35 mm gap and 0.20 mm clearance. Native custom rules require at most
0.15 mm skew, 70 mm length and 6 mm uncoupled extent. These are agent-selected
qualification assumptions, not CAN product requirements. No impedance,
return-path, circuit, assembly or manufacturing acceptance is claimed.

The unmodified router leaves about 2.784 mm skew. Matching at 1 mm amplitude
is insufficient; 2 mm permits a compliant route without changing the input.
The tests independently inspect saved copper and native checks.
